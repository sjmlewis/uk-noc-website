Offset news

A weekly round-up of new defence sales around the world above the thresholds at which countries require Offset or industrial participation, and changes to those rules. Each summary is written from public reports.

Newest first. Updated weekly. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Week of 23 November 202613 items

  • United Kingdom24 November 2026

    UK awards HII £142m for REMUS 620 mine-hunting drones for the Royal Navy

    The UK's National Armaments Director Group has awarded US shipbuilder HII a £142 million (about US$186 million) contract for REMUS 620 uncrewed underwater vehicles, Sea Launcher launch and recovery systems and support services for the Royal Navy's Mine-Hunting Capability programme. HII says support work previously done overseas will move to the UK. The contract was placed before the UK's offset policy takes effect in April 2027.

  • Poland24 November 2026

    Poland and the UK sign an agreement to co-develop the CAMM-MR air defence missile

    Poland and the UK signed an intergovernmental agreement in Gdynia on 24 November setting out how they will co-operate on developing the medium-range CAMM-MR missile, planned as a key part of Poland's layered air and missile defence. It is to lead to a contract for PGZ and MBDA UK to produce the missile's detailed design.

  • Bulgaria23 November 2026

    Leonardo to upgrade the avionics of Bulgaria's three C-27J Spartan transport aircraft

    Leonardo announced on 23 November that it will upgrade the avionics of the Bulgarian Air Force's three C-27J Spartan aircraft, with a redesigned crew interface, new navigation and surveillance equipment and upgraded communications. No value or timetable has been disclosed.

  • Thailand20 November 2026

    Thailand seeks to buy HIMARS rocket launchers from the US, worth up to US$123m

    The White House says Thailand intends to commit up to US$123 million (about THB 4.2 billion) to buy Lockheed Martin's HIMARS rocket artillery system. Thailand's Ministry of Defence confirmed on 20 November that a proposal has been made to the US government and that the purchase is at an early stage.

  • Netherlands19 November 2026

    Netherlands and Germany order Knifefish jammers, with a Dutch industrial participation deal

    The Dutch procurement agency COMMIT, signing for both countries, ordered series production of Hensoldt Netherlands' Knifefish radio-jamming systems at the NEDS fair in Rotterdam on 19 November, for fitting to Piranha 5 armoured vehicles. Hensoldt also signed an industrial participation agreement with the Dutch Ministry of Economic Affairs to bring Dutch companies and research institutions into the programme. No value has been published.

  • Netherlands20 November 2026

    Netherlands takes first step to join the Finland-led CAVS programme for Patria 6x6 vehicles

    The Netherlands has signed a Technical Arrangement in Rotterdam as the first step towards becoming the eighth participant in the Common Armoured Vehicle System programme for Patria 6x6 armoured vehicles. No Dutch quantity or value has been published.

  • NetherlandsRules20 November 2026

    Dutch defence industry plan gives more weight to where products and components come from

    The Netherlands' new defence industry plan says the Ministry of Defence will give more weight to the origin of products and suppliers in procurement decisions from 2027, and from early 2028 its guidelines will take account of where raw materials and components come from. The plan does not change the Dutch industrial participation rules.

  • Bulgaria19 November 2026

    Bulgaria's Parliament approves a €696m purchase of 57 CAESAR howitzers from France

    Bulgaria's Parliament voted on 19 November to approve a deal worth about €696 million for 57 CAESAR 155mm self-propelled howitzers from France, with additional components, funded through the EU's SAFE loans and the defence budget. Delivery is planned to finish by the end of 2030.

  • Switzerland19 November 2026

    Switzerland picks Thales Suisse for deployable Ground Master 200 air surveillance radars

    armasuisse has chosen Thales Suisse SA to supply deployable medium-range Ground Master 200 radars for the Swiss Air Force, replacing the TAFLIR system, after inviting bids from four European manufacturers. armasuisse cites greater Swiss industry involvement and technology transfer for radar maintenance; no value has been published.

  • India18 November 2026

    India signs Rs 1,976 crore contract with IAI to upgrade the Navy's Barak missile system

    India's Ministry of Defence signed a Rs 1,976.40 crore contract with Israel Aerospace Industries on 18 November to upgrade the hardware and software of the Barak point defence missile system on Indian Navy ships. The value is just below India's Rs 2,000 crore offset threshold, so no offset applies.

  • MoroccoRules18 November 2026

    Morocco launches a defence industry roadmap aiming to meet 40% of its needs locally by 2035

    King Mohammed VI chaired a session in Rabat on 18 November launching the Morocco Offer, a national roadmap for the defence and security industry that aims to meet about 40% of the country's military and security needs locally by 2035.

  • Malaysia17 November 2026

    Malaysia and France sign defence agreements, including 105mm artillery and a Scorpene upgrade

    During President Macron's visit to Kuala Lumpur on 17 November, Malaysia and France signed seven letters of intent and three memoranda of understanding, including a proposed purchase of a KNDS 105mm light artillery system and work on the Navy's Scorpene submarine mid-life upgrade. Malaysia's defence minister said the Rafale is among the options for its future combat aircraft; no values were published.

  • Denmark5 November 2026

    Denmark directly awards Saab a framework for laser duel training systems worth up to SEK 4.75bn

    Denmark's Defence Acquisition and Logistics Organisation has directly awarded Saab a framework agreement for more BT46 instrumented laser duel simulation units and their servicing, estimated at SEK 3.75 billion and worth up to SEK 4.75 billion. It runs from November 2026 to January 2037, with a five-year extension option.

Week of 16 November 202610 items

  • Poland13 November 2026

    Pentagon backs Poland's request to buy Tomahawk cruise missiles and ground launchers

    Poland's defence minister said on 13 November that the Pentagon has given a favourable recommendation to Poland's request for Tomahawk cruise missiles and ground-based launchers; US congressional approval is still needed. No value has been published.

  • Malaysia12 November 2026

    Malaysia picks Roketsan's ATMACA to replace NSM on its Littoral Combat Ships

    Malaysia's defence minister named the Turkish ATMACA anti-ship missile on 12 November as the preferred replacement for the Naval Strike Missile on the Navy's Littoral Combat Ships, with talks with Türkiye to follow. No quantity or value has been published.

  • United Kingdom12 November 2026

    UK to buy three offshore support vessels through Norway's standardised vessel programme

    The UK and Norway agreed in Oslo on 12 November that the Royal Navy will buy three offshore support vessels for uncrewed mine-hunting, with an option for three more, through Norway's P1118 programme; the UK has set aside an extra £90 million and the first ship is due in 2031. As a government-to-government purchase it is normally exempt from separate offset negotiations under the UK's offset policy.

  • Bulgaria11 November 2026

    Bulgaria approves joint purchase with Italy of up to 1,040 military transport vehicles

    Bulgaria's Cabinet approved on 11 November a joint purchase with Italy of up to 1,040 military transport vehicles for up to €410 million, with the first stage of up to 700 vehicles (up to €290 million, 2027 to 2030) funded through the EU's SAFE loans. Part of the equipment is to be assembled in Bulgaria with Bulgarian industry.

  • Denmark11 November 2026

    Denmark orders SEK 350m of CV90 live training equipment from Saab

    Denmark's Defence Acquisition and Logistics Organisation has ordered further live training equipment for its CV90 vehicles from Saab, including a laser duel system and drone and indirect-fire training, worth SEK 350 million (about US$35 million). Deliveries run from 2026 to 2028.

  • Belgium9 November 2026

    Belgium orders 20 Rheinmetall Skyranger 30 air defence systems

    Belgium has ordered 20 Skyranger 30 air defence systems from Rheinmetall, with logistics, as part of its Blue Dome air defence programme, in a contract in the mid-hundreds of millions of euros. Deliveries are due to start in late 2028.

  • Peru6 November 2026

    Peru's Interior Ministry awards Katmerciler US$24.4m for 44 police water-cannon trucks

    Peru's Ministry of the Interior has awarded Turkey's Katmerciler a US$24.4 million contract for 44 water-cannon trucks for the National Police, with training and up to 60 months of preventive maintenance.

  • Brazil5 November 2026

    Brazilian Army chooses MBDA's EMADS medium-range air defence, worth up to R$3.4bn

    The Brazilian Army has chosen MBDA's EMADS system with CAMM-ER missiles for three medium-range air defence batteries, with six launcher vehicles and four radar vehicles, at a reported cost of up to R$3.4 billion. The purchase still depends on commercial and government-to-government negotiations.

  • LithuaniaRules4 November 2026

    Lithuania to write named local suppliers into its infantry fighting vehicle contract

    Lithuania's government approved a draft resolution on 4 November under which domestic industry participation in its tracked infantry fighting vehicle purchase, about 90 to 100 vehicles, would be a requirement of the supply contract itself, naming Lithuanian suppliers and their work, instead of a separate industrial cooperation agreement.

  • Greece2 November 2026

    Greece signs for three Embraer KC-390 transport aircraft through Portugal

    Greece signed in Athens on 2 November for three Embraer KC-390 Millennium aircraft, with options for three more, in a government-to-government deal through Portugal, reported at about €590 million for the first three. The first aircraft is due in 2027.

Week of 9 November 20267 items

  • South Korea6 November 2026

    South Korea selects the Sikorsky S-92A+ for its new VIP command helicopters

    South Korea's Defense Acquisition Program Promotion Committee chose the Sikorsky S-92A+ on 6 November for its next command helicopter programme, with a total cost of about KRW 873 billion. Four helicopters will replace older Air Force aircraft by 2031, and the plan includes modification and maintenance technology and parts exports for Korean small firms.

  • Mexico6 November 2026

    US approves a possible US$1.075bn sale of 11 UH-60M Black Hawk helicopters to Mexico

    The US State Department has approved a possible sale to Mexico of 11 Sikorsky UH-60M Black Hawk helicopters with spare engines, navigation systems, radios, training and support, estimated at US$1.075 billion.

  • Sweden6 November 2026

    Saab wins about SEK 2bn from Naval Group to equip Sweden's four new FDI frigates

    Naval Group has ordered weapon control systems and sensors worth about SEK 2 billion from Saab for the four FDI frigates it is building for the Royal Swedish Navy under a contract of about €4.3 billion signed on 31 August. Sweden does not have offset rules.

  • Denmark6 November 2026

    Denmark and Lithuania join Finland-led joint purchase of Blocker naval mines

    Denmark and Lithuania have confirmed they are buying Forcit Defence's Blocker naval mines through the five-nation Naval Mines Cooperation, under a framework contract Finland signed in June for the group. Finland has described the contract as worth hundreds of millions of euros; no figure per country has been published.

  • Philippines5 November 2026

    India reported in final talks to sell the Philippines more BrahMos missile systems

    India is reported to be in final-stage talks to expand its BrahMos supersonic cruise missile sale to the Philippines, covering about five more systems and nearly 60 missiles, which Indian sources value at US$700–800 million. No deal has yet been announced.

  • MalaysiaCivil5 November 2026

    Penang Mutiara Line LRT: RM2.69bn second civil contract awarded

    Malaysia Rapid Transit Corporation has awarded the RM2.69 billion second civil main contract for the Penang Mutiara Line light rail to a joint venture of China Communications Construction Company, TRC Construction and Ufuk Semarak. It covers about 5.5 km of line, including a 3.05 km sea-crossing bridge to Butterworth.

  • United KingdomRules4 November 2026

    UK offset details expected at the Defence Investment Summit on 1 December

    Details of how obligations under the UK's new offset policy will be assessed, measured and enforced are expected at the Defence Investment Summit on 1 December. The policy is due to apply from April 2027 to contracts of £50 million or more, with each case considered individually.
